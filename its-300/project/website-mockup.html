Color palette

The color scheme is a fundamental component of the corporate identity that helps to ensure consistent & coherent communications.

Use this color palette when designing Estrera Botanicals products & collaterals. Most print applications will be CMYK & spot color and all electronic use will be RGB or Hexadecimal.

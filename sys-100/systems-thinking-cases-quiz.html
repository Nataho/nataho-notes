Systems Thinking Cases Quiz

Multiple-choice self-test covering Cases 01–12 from SYS 100. One question per case. Check one option per question and write your final answer in the answer field.

Score: 7/12 - 58%
1. (Case 01 — It's Global Warming, Stupid) According to the Munich Re report, weather-related loss events in which region nearly quintupled from 1980–2011, with $1.06 trillion in losses?
  • A. Asia
  • B. North America
  • C. Europe
  • D. Africa

Answer:

1. Correct - North America's weather-related loss events nearly quintupled from 1980–2011, with $1.06 trillion in losses (Case 01, Munich Re report).
2. (Case 02 — The Global Obesity Bomb) Which countries already had higher obesity rates than the United States at the time of the article?
  • A. Panama, Saudi Arabia, and six Pacific Island nations
  • B. Brazil and Mexico only
  • C. France and Germany
  • D. Japan and South Korea

Answer:

2. Wrong - you picked Brazil and Mexico only; correct is A. Panama, Saudi Arabia, and six Pacific Island nations, because the Case 02 notes list these as the upstart challengers that already have higher obesity rates than the U.S.
3. (Case 03 — The Financial Crisis Blame Game) In 2004 the SEC loosened its rules, allowing investment banks to increase leverage from 10–15x to what level?
  • A. 20–25x
  • B. 30–40x
  • C. 50–60x
  • D. 100x

Answer:

3. Correct - the SEC loosened rules in 2004, allowing investment banks to leverage 30–40x core holdings, up from 10–15x (Case 03).
4. (Case 04 — Airport Security Is Killing Us) According to the article, what were the odds that a U.S. resident would die in a terrorist attack since 2000?
  • A. 1 in 10,000
  • B. 1 in 150,000
  • C. 1 in 500,000
  • D. 1 in 3.5 million

Answer:

4. Correct - the odds a U.S. resident dies in a terrorist attack were 1 in 3.5 million since 2000 (Case 04).
5. (Case 05 — Death or Debt Confound Egyptians) What was the amount of the IMF loan to Egypt that remained unagreed during Mursi's first 100 days?
  • A. $1.5B
  • B. $4.8B
  • C. $10B
  • D. $25B

Answer:

5. Correct - a $4.8B IMF loan remained unagreed during Mursi's first 100 days (Case 05).
6. (Case 06 — Pakistan Tries New Way of Tackling Corruption) According to Transparency International, Pakistan slipped nine places to become which ranked country on the corruption index?
  • A. 12th most corrupt of 176
  • B. 47th most corrupt of 176
  • C. 33rd most corrupt of 176
  • D. 100th most corrupt of 176

Answer:

6. Wrong - you picked 47th most corrupt of 176; correct is C. 33rd most corrupt of 176, because the Case 06 notes say Pakistan slipped nine places to become the 33rd most corrupt of 176 countries (Transparency International).
7. (Case 07 — The End of China's One-Child Policy?) In what year was China's one-child policy put in place?
  • A. 1966
  • B. 1976
  • C. 1989
  • D. 1979

Answer:

7. Wrong - you picked 1976; correct is D. 1979, because the Case 07 notes state the one-child policy has been in place since 1979 (the mindset only traces back to shortages just after the Cultural Revolution ended in 1976).
8. (Case 08 — U.S. Grads Work as Waiters While Italy's Remain Jobless) What was Italy's 15–24 youth unemployment rate in July 2012?
  • A. 16.8%
  • B. 22.5%
  • C. 35.3%
  • D. 42.0%

Answer:

8. Correct - Italy's 15–24 jobless rate hit 35.3% in July 2012 (Case 08; note that 16.8% was the U.S. rate and 22.5% was the EU-wide rate).
9. (Case 09 — Amazon Wins Dismissal of Apple's False Advertising Claim) Which judge dismissed Apple's false advertising claim against Amazon?
  • A. Kimba Wood
  • B. Phyllis Hamilton
  • C. Lucy Koh
  • D. Denise Cote

Answer:

9. Correct - U.S. District Judge Phyllis Hamilton in Oakland, California dismissed Apple's false advertising claim against Amazon (Case 09).
10. (Case 10 — Kodak Files for Bankruptcy as Digital Era Spells End to Film) In what year did Kodak invent the first digital camera, which it then shelved?
  • A. 1959
  • B. 1975
  • C. 1988
  • D. 1991

Answer:

10. Wrong - you picked 1988; correct is B. 1975, because the Case 10 notes say Kodak invented the first digital camera in 1975 and shelved it to protect its lucrative film business.
11. (Case 11 — J&J Again Recalls Thousands of Faulty Hip Implants) What did the U.K. joint registry find about the recalled Adept hip implants?
  • A. 50% of patients needed replacement within two years
  • B. 30% of patients needed replacement within one year
  • C. 7.1% of patients needed replacement within three years
  • D. 12.1% of patients needed replacement within seven years

Answer:

11. Correct - the U.K. joint registry found 12.1% of patients needed replacement within seven years (Case 11; the 7.1% within three years figure is for the Australian registry).
12. (Case 12 — Arch Coal Laying Off 750 Workers in Appalachia) Coal's share of U.S. electricity was forecast to fall below what percentage for the year — the lowest since data collection began in 1949?
  • A. 50%
  • B. 40%
  • C. 30%
  • D. 25%

Answer:

12. Wrong - you picked 30%; correct is B. 40%, because the Case 12 notes forecast coal's share of U.S. electricity to fall below 40% for the year (the lowest since 1949); it was seen reaching ~30% only by the end of the decade.

Brief History of BPO in the Philippines

Last updated: Nov 14, 2026

📞 Brief History of BPO in the Philippines

⚡ TL;DR (read this in 15 seconds)
  • 1992 → first contact center (Accenture)
  • 2010 → Philippines becomes the BPO capital of the world
  • 2024 → 850+ BPO companies
  • 2030 → AI roadmap targets a $92 billion GDP contribution

🧍 The Pioneers (who made it happen)

Rainier "Bong" Borja

Often regarded as the most articulate voice of the Philippine BPO industry.

Karen Batungbacal

Early involvement and a series of contact center firms.

Secretary Mar Roxas

As DTI Secretary, saw the industry's potential, promoted foreign investment, and helped shape the BPO landscape.

Why it matters

The IT-BPO sector was not an overnight success — these individuals saw the potential early.

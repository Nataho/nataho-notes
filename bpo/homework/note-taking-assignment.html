This cluster of acronyms heavily represents the IT & Business Process Management (IT-BPM) sector, global shared services, and standard business operations.

Industry Associations & Governing Bodies

  • IBPAP – IT and Business Process Association of the Philippines

    • The primary umbrella association and trade organization for the IT-BPM sector in the Philippines, working with government and academia to drive industry growth.

  • PSIA – Philippine Software Industry Association

    • An industry association representing software development, tech solutions, and IT service companies.

  • GDAP – Game Developers Association of the Philippines

    • The organization representing local game design, interactive media, and digital entertainment studios.

  • CCAP – Contact Center Association of the Philippines

    • The trade association representing contact centers, call centers, and customer experience operations.

  • HIMAP – Healthcare Information Management Association of the Philippines

    • An association representing companies providing health IT, medical coding, transcription, and healthcare BPO services.

  • GICC – Global In-House Center Council

    • The industry association representing captive corporate shared service centers and global capability centers.akoa

  • ACPI – Animation Council of the Philippines, Inc.

    • The trade group representing animation studios, 2D/3D visual artists, and post-production service providers.

Business Models, Sectors & Programs

  • GCC – Global Capability Center (Primary IT-BPM Context)

    • An in-house, offshore/nearshore facility established by a global company to perform core operations, engineering, IT, or analytics tasks internally.

    • (Alternative Macroeconomic Context: Gulf Cooperation Council)

  • IT-BMP / IT-BPM – Information Technology and Business Process Management

    • The overarching business sector that delivers technology services, back-office operations, and customer support via outsourcing or shared services. (Also referred to as IT-BMP for IT-Business Management Program/Process).

  • BPS – Business Process Services

    • Outsourced operational tasks and functions (e.g., HR, accounting, customer care) managed by an external provider or internal shared service team.

  • SMP – Service Management Program (IT-BPM Context)

    • An industry-designed academic track integrated into university curricula to prepare students for management and operational roles in the IT-BPM sector.

    • (Alternative Marketing Context: Social Media Platform / Social Media Plan)

Legal, Service Standards & Metrics

  • SLA – Service Level Agreement

    • A formal contract defining specific service performance standards (e.g., response time, resolution rate, system uptime) between a service provider and client.

  • NDA – Non-Disclosure Agreement

    • A legal contract designed to protect confidential business details, client data, and proprietary trade secrets from unauthorized disclosure.

  • TAT – Turnaround Time

    • The total elapsed time required to process, complete, and deliver a task or request from start to finish.

Operational Roles & Functional Units

  • HR – Human Resources

    • The business unit handling recruitment, payroll, employee management, training, and corporate policy.

  • VA – Virtual Assistant

    • A remote administrative or specialist contractor providing virtual support to a business or individual. (Also refers to Voice Analytics in contact center tech).

  • OM – Operations Manager

    • A managerial role overseeing daily team workflows, delivery quality, efficiency, and operational execution.

  • TSR – Technical Support Representative

    • A frontline customer service specialist trained to troubleshoot technical, network, hardware, or software issues.

  • CSR – Customer Service Representative / Corporate Social Responsibility

    • Operations Role: Customer Service Representative – A agent handling customer inquiries, orders, and issue resolution.
    • Corporate Governance: Corporate Social Responsibility – Business practices intended to make a positive impact on society and the environment.

  • SEO – Search Engine Optimization

    • Digital marketing techniques aimed at improving website content visibility and ranking on search engine result pages.

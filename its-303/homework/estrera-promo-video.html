Estrera Promo Video

#homework#taskLast updated: Nov 12, 2026

task: 1-minute promotional video for the Estrera Botanicals website (the Midterm Website project) — show the shop contents + the customer features up to checkout, end on a pleasant brand message.
brand guide: Website Mockup (its palette sections are placeholders, so the real shipped colors below are the source of truth)

the story in one line

browse → want it → one tap → pay your way → it's on its way → close.
one action per shot, nine shots, exactly 60 seconds. no login screens, no error states, no admin tools — only the customer journey.

the brand kit (as actually shipped)

fonts (the shipped ones, not the guide's placeholders): DM Serif Display for headings + Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else. both are free Google Fonts and match the recorded site exactly, so captions look native.

shot 7 pipeline overlay

the summary page shows the status badge; the six-step animated pipeline is an overlay you animate in the editor on top of the real page — it visualizes the site's real status pipeline. that's the animation-class showpiece, so spend the most love there.

shot details (what to actually click)

  1. intro card — don't record anything; build it in the editor. wordmark centered, tagline at 40% size, 1.5s before the crossfade starts.
  2. home hero — start with the page already at top, cursor idle 0.5s so the zoom reads, then drift slowly (no fast scroll).
  3. home below — scroll at a steady "reading" pace; folder the pause at "Skincare Essentials" for ~1s so the 3 product cards register, then click SHOP at 0:17.
  4. botanical shop — the grid headliners to frame: Midnight Recovery Oil ₱140 · Chebula Active Serum ₱200 · Flora Shield ₱150 · Chebula Extreme Cream ₱110 · Blooming Mama Oil ₱54. cursor travels down each card ~0.8s apart.
  5. add to cart — target: Midnight Recovery Oil (the prettiest bottle). modal opens at 0:28, confirm at 0:31, watch the floating badge tick 1.
  6. checkout — address card is pre-selected (demo account autofill). click GCash (details expand) → Credit/Debit Card (masked •••• — good sign, keep it on screen only masked) → Cash on Delivery. Place Order Now → "Confirm & Place Order" modal click at 0:44.
  7. order summary — let the real page hold for 2s, then the overlay pipeline walks through all 6 statuses (1 chip per second).
  8. my account — land on My Orders with the "Pending" chip; a 1s pan over the sidebar tabs (My Orders · Addresses · Payment Options) tells the story "there's a whole account area".
  9. end card — mission/vision text (the brand guide's is still ==Null==, this doubles for ITS 300 later):

    Estrera Botanicals exists to bring honest, plant-made skincare from raw botanicals to Filipino doorsteps — gently made, fairly priced, delivered with care.

recording pre-flight checklist

  • demo data: log in as a demo customer with a saved "Home" address + GCash number (so checkout autofills on camera); cart empty
  • privacy: fake-looking demo address/name on camera — nothing real
  • browser: fullscreen F11, 100% zoom, bookmarks bar hidden, one tab only, 1920×1080
  • recorder (OBS Studio, free): Window capture of the browser, 30–60 fps, mic muted (music added in edit)
  • cursor discipline: slow deliberate moves; 2–3 takes of the whole click-through, keep the smoothest
  • the order created during recording stays pending in the dev DB — harmless, ignore

edit recipe

  • transitions: 0.4–0.5s crossfades between shots 1→9; inside shot 6 the cuts on each payment tile can be straight (0.3s) to feel snappy
  • captions: DM Serif Display, 56–72px, slide-up + fade over 8–10 frames, positioned lower-third; keep them ≥1.2s on screen; sentence case, no full stops
  • pipeline overlay (shot 7): six chips, light green #83A27E circles that pop with a 105% overshoot and turn dark green #2B502E with a white check as each status completes
  • principles hook (links to 12 Principles of Animation): every shot has one focal point (navigating the eye), all moves use ease-in/ease-out, and the modal pop + chip overshoots give you follow-through. worth naming in your writeup.
  • music: calm acoustic or lo-fi bed from the YT Audio Library (search "Morning" / "Bloom" vibes), ~20% volume, fade out under the end card
  • export: MP4 H.264, 1920×1080, 30 fps, ≤60s, audio at normal level

optional voiceover (skip it — captions carry the story)

if you ever want VO, read this slow over the board:

Plants, bottled. (0:05) Welcome to Estrera Botanicals — skincare made straight from raw plant botanicals. (0:12) Five formulas, one ritual — oils, serums and creams for sensitive days. (0:22) Found one you like? One tap, and it's yours. (0:30) Pay your way — GCash, card, or cash on delivery. (0:38) And from the moment it leaves our hands, you can track it — every step, until it reaches your door. (0:47) Everything lives in your account. (0:52) Estrera Botanicals. Grown with care, delivered with love. (1:00)

submit checklist

  • length 60s ±3s
  • captions spellchecked (they're on screen longest — errors live there)
  • no real personal data visible on any screen
  • first/last frame are brand cards (never a half-loaded page)
  • export checked on a phone screen once — captions still readable?

the blend rule (what makes capture→capture transitions clean):

  • capture→capture fades draw both layers at the same zoom/focus so the backgrounds align and only the new element (modal, panel) dissolves in
  • same-image zooms are a single animated draw (one Ken Burns layer) — never a crossfade of two framings of one page, that double-exposes everything
  • where it was applied: s8 single push zoom 1→1.24 · s5 grid settles from s4's exact framing before its modal dissolves · s6 confirm drawn at checkout's end framing while the page dims

v4 — the captured pages rebuilt as living elements (as actually built)

what changed vs v3: the verdict on v3 was "a slideshow" — static captures
with zooms and dissolves. v4 keeps the same 9 shots, timings, captions, browser
frame and journey (Blooming Mama Oil ₱54 → checkout → order #00009 Pending),
but every captured page is rebuilt from scratch as living UI — nav, product
cards, buttons, payment tiles, modals are drawn element-by-element each frame,
so the interactions are real: a cursor travels/hovers/presses (dip + ripple on
every click), cards lift under hover, the cart and confirm modals pop with
overshoot, radio options fill on selection, and the home/checkout pages scroll
as actual content with a sticky nav. the 12 screenshots are the blueprint, not
the footage.

fixed in the QC loop (the bugs a still-image pass caught):

  • cursor teleported between keys → rewritten hold-then-travel (each key time is the arrival; holds 45% of the gap, then easedIO arc)

  • modal dim painted outside the content clip → covered the whole canvas; now clipped to the viewport
